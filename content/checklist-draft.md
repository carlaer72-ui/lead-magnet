# The 5-Step Sandwich Shop Pre-Launch Checklist

## 1. Nail Your Menu
- Narrow down to 6–10 signature sandwiches — don't try to do everything on day one.
- Test every recipe multiple times for consistency (same result, different day, different hands).
- Lock in suppliers for bread, deli meats, and produce; confirm pricing holds at your target margin.

## 2. Health & Safety Compliance
- Get your food handler permits and any required staff certifications.
- Schedule and pass your health department inspection.
- Verify kitchen equipment (slicers, fridges, prep surfaces) meets local code.

## 3. Set Up Your Supply Chain
- Confirm delivery schedules with your bread, meat, and produce suppliers.
- Line up backup vendors in case a supplier falls through.
- Order packaging (wrap, bags, boxes) with enough lead time before opening.

## 4. Train Your Team
- Run the team through the full assembly line until it's fast and consistent.
- Cover food safety basics: cross-contamination, storage temps, handwashing.
- Make sure everyone's comfortable on the POS/register before day one.

## 5. Soft Launch & Gather Feedback
- Do a friends-and-family tasting before the real opening.
- Build a little buzz on social media in the week leading up to launch.
- Plan an opening-day promo, and have a way to collect customer feedback from day one.
